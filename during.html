Stay Alert.Keep Yourself Safe.

Conditions can change quickly. Listen to official
instructions and avoid taking risks to protect
property.

DURING THE TYPHOON
01

Stay Indoors

Remain in a sturdy part of the house and keep away from windows and glass. Move
to an interior room if winds become strong.

02

Keep Updated

Listen to a battery powered radio or check reliable official updates when safe.
Keep your phone available for urgent communication.

03

Evacuate When Told

Follow evacuation orders promptly. Bring your emergency kit and use the
designated safe route; do not delay to collect belongings.

04

Avoid Floodwater

Never walk, swim, or drive through floodwater. It may be deeper or faster than it
looks and can hide dangerous hazards.
