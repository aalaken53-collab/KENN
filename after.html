AFTER THE TYPHOON
01

Wait for the All-Clear

Do not return to an evacuated area or inspect damage until local authorities say it
is safe.

02

Watch for Hazards

Stay away from fallen power lines, damaged buildings, unstable trees, and flooded
roads. Report dangerous conditions.

03

Check Your Home Safely

Look for damage from a safe position. Have qualified personnel inspect electrical
or gas hazards before using affected systems.

04

Use Safe Food and Water

Follow local drinking-water advisories. Do not consume food that touched
floodwater or may have spoiled.
