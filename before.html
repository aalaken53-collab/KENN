Prepare Today.Stay Safe Tomorrow.

Taking simple precautions before a typhoon arrives
can help protect your family, home, and belongings.

BEFORE THE TYPHOON
01

Pack an Emergency Kit

Prepare drinking water, ready to eat food, needed medicines, a first-aid kit,
flashlight, batteries, radio, hygiene supplies, cash, and important documents.

02

Make a Family Plan

Agree on a meeting place, choose an emergency contact, save important phone
numbers, and identify your evacuation center and safe routes.

03

Secure Your Home

Clear gutters and drains, bring loose outdoor items inside, protect windows, and
move important belongings to a higher place if flooding is possible.

04

Monitor Official Updates

Follow PAGASA and local government announcements. Know the warning signals
and evacuate early when authorities advise it.
